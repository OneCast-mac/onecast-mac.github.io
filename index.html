How It Works

Step 1: Connect

Ensure your Xbox One is on your home network (LAN recommended) and sign in with your Xbox Live gamertag to register the console.

Step 2: Pair Controller

Connect your Xbox One wireless controller to your Mac via USB or Bluetooth. Stay within range of the console for direct wireless link.

Step 3: Launch and Play

Open OneCast, select your game or profile, and stream in HD. Adjust settings like video quality or display mode as needed.

Step 4: Enjoy Seamlessly

Switch to full screen, monitor stats like video height and lag. Version 3.4 includes stability improvements and default "Hide not owned games."

FAQ

Do I need to be near the Xbox?

Your controller must connect directly to the console wirelessly, so stay within range. The Mac streams over your network.

What networks work best?

Xbox One on LAN cable, Mac on 5GHz WiFi for optimal low-lag streaming.

Can I use multiple consoles?

Yes, profiles support any number of Xbox One consoles or Xbox Live gamertags.

Is setup complicated?

One-time setup takes seconds. Sign in once, and you're ready to stream.
